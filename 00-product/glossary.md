# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Guest | A person staying at, visiting, or buying from the centre. |
| Lead | An unqualified request or potential customer before a booking exists. |
| Stay | The occupied period of a confirmed room booking. |
| Booking | A commercial commitment for accommodation and/or services. |
| Experience | A bookable guest activity: yoga, sauna, massage, event or excursion. |
| Retreat | A multi-day programme with participants, itinerary and potentially accommodation. |
| Day event | A scheduled single-day programme, open to residents and/or external participants. |
| Service order | A request fulfilled by a team, such as food, transfer or housekeeping. |
| Capacity | Sellable supply: rooms, seats, time slots, staff, equipment or vehicles. |
| AI agent | A policy-bounded software worker that may decide, propose or execute work. |
| Override | A human decision replacing an AI proposal or automated outcome. |
