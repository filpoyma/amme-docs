# Vision

AMME is not a conventional PMS/ERP with a chat assistant attached. It is an **AI-first hospitality operating system** for a high-touch retreat centre.

The transactional core keeps trustworthy records of guests, stays, money, inventory and capacity. AI agents propose and execute bounded operational work: answer leads, assemble quotes, coordinate arrivals, plan food, fill activities and flag exceptions. People retain authority over policy, money, safety, guest care and exceptional decisions.

## Product principles

1. **Hospitality before administration.** The system should give staff more time with guests, not create another control panel.
2. **AI with accountable autonomy.** Every consequential decision has a policy, evidence, confidence, execution record and human escalation path.
3. **One operational picture.** Room, kitchen, wellness and transport planning share real availability rather than disconnected calendars.
4. **Small-centre realism.** Optimise for ten rooms and a compact team before adding enterprise complexity.
5. **Events before screens.** The domain model represents what happens in the centre; interfaces follow from it.
