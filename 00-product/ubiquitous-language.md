# Ubiquitous Language

Use these terms consistently in product, operations and engineering conversations.

- Say **room** for a sellable accommodation unit; do not alternate it with *villa* unless villas become a distinct product type.
- Say **booking** for the commitment and **stay** for the actual occupied period.
- Say **participant** for an attendee of a retreat or event; the participant may also be a guest.
- Say **experience** for a bookable activity; **session** is its scheduled occurrence.
- Say **AI proposal** before a decision is approved or executed; say **AI action** only after it has acted.
- Say **human override** when staff replace AI behaviour. It must include a reason for consequential actions.
- Say **availability** for capacity that can still be offered, and **allocation** for capacity assigned to a booking.
