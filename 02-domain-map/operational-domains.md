# Operational Domains

These are the day-to-day work areas that must share dependable facts while retaining ownership boundaries.

| Work area | Primary modules | Typical outcomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sales and guest care | CRM, Booking, Finance, Notifications | lead qualified, quote sent, booking confirmed, payment requested |
| Arrival and departure | Booking, Accommodation, Staff & operations | room allocated, check-in completed, checkout completed |
| Room turnaround | Accommodation, Staff & operations | cleaning requested, room ready, maintenance resolved |
| Guest services | Wellness, Kitchen & F&B, Transfers & excursions | appointment delivered, order served, transfer completed |
| Retreat/event operations | Retreats & events, Booking, Wellness, Kitchen & F&B | participant registered, programme delivered, attendance recorded |
| Purchasing and stock | Inventory & procurement, Kitchen & F&B, Finance | stock received, stock consumed, purchase approved |
| Daily control | Finance, Staff & operations, Analytics | expenses recorded, shift completed, daily close reviewed |

The daily board is a read model spanning these modules. It is not a new owner of their work.
