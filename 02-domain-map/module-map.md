# Module Map

| Module | Purpose | Main concepts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity & access | Authenticate actors and authorise actions. | User, role, permission, session |
| CRM | Maintain the relationship with leads and guests. | Lead, guest, conversation, preference, document, loyalty |
| Booking | Manage the commercial commitment and its lifecycle. | Booking, booking guest, reservation item, stay, allocation request |
| Accommodation | Operate rooms and their operational readiness. | Room, room category, room allocation, housekeeping, maintenance |
| Service catalogue & scheduling | Describe sellable offerings and schedulable capacity. | Service, experience, session, resource, availability |
| Wellness & activities | Deliver massage, sauna, yoga and similar sessions. | Appointment, therapist assignment, attendance |
| Kitchen & F&B | Manage menu, guest orders and kitchen fulfilment. | Menu item, order, kitchen ticket, table/service delivery |
| Retreats & events | Run multi-day retreats and day events. | Retreat, programme, participant, event, registration |
| Transfers & excursions | Plan transport and external experiences. | Transfer, excursion, itinerary, provider assignment |
| Inventory & procurement | Control stock, suppliers and purchases. | Product, stock movement, supplier, purchase |
| Finance | Record money owed, received and spent. | Invoice, payment, refund, expense, cashflow, daily close |
| Staff & operations | Coordinate people, shifts and operational work. | Employee, shift, task, checklist, daily board |
| Notifications & communications | Deliver outbound messages through channels. | Notification, delivery, template, channel |
| Files & documents | Store files and generated documents with access rules. | File, document, attachment |
| Automation | Evaluate approved rules and run auditable workflows. | Rule, trigger, execution, action |
| AI workforce | Run specialised agents within policy boundaries. | Agent, proposal, action, tool call, evaluation |
| Analytics | Provide read-only operational and financial insight. | KPI, report, projection, forecast |

`Service catalogue & scheduling` owns generic sellability and capacity. Delivery modules own their specialised execution. For example, the catalogue defines a massage offering; Wellness owns the appointment and treatment outcome.
