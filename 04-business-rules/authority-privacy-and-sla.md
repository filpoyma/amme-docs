# Authority, Privacy and Service Levels

## Mandatory human approval

Human approval is mandatory for guest-profile deletion or merge, reservation deletion, invoice void/correction, refunds above limit, tax-rate change, manual day close, reopening a closed day, significant damage write-off, significant complimentary service, price override above limit, manual occupancy override, security-deposit change and sensitive-data access/export.

## Sensitive-data access

| Role | Permitted scope |
| --- | --- |
| Reception | Check-in data required for their work |
| Manager | Full operational access |
| Finance | Financial information; no wellness data unless specifically necessary |
| Housekeeping | Room/task details only; no passport or finance data |
| Wellness staff | Procedure-relevant wellness information only |

Passport, finance and wellness data export is prohibited by default. Only Manager/Admin may export it; every export is logged.

## Service-level targets

| Event | Response / completion target |
| --- | --- |
| New lead | ≤15 minutes during working hours |
| Booking request | ≤30 minutes |
| Hold confirmation | ≤30 minutes |
| Room turnaround after checkout | ≤60 minutes |
| Critical maintenance | Response ≤10 minutes |
| High maintenance | Response ≤30 minutes |
| Normal maintenance | Response ≤4 hours |
| Critical guest complaint | Response ≤10 minutes |
| Normal complaint | Response ≤30 minutes |
| Final complaint response | ≤24 hours |

For critical maintenance, the target is response/start of handling, not guaranteed resolution within ten minutes.
