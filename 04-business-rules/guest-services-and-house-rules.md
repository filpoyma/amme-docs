# Guest Safety, Services and House Rules

## Wellness review

The system does not diagnose. It marks an appointment `Needs Specialist Review` before confirmation when the guest reports pregnancy relevant to a procedure, acute infectious illness, fever, open wounds, severe allergy, alcohol/drug intoxication or another procedure-specific contraindication. A qualified specialist decides whether to proceed, offer an alternative or request medical clearance.

## Allergens

Kitchen must manually confirm handling for peanuts, tree nuts, shellfish, fish, dairy, eggs, gluten/wheat, soy and sesame. For a severe allergy or anaphylaxis report, a POS flag is insufficient; kitchen records `Confirmed Safe`, `Cannot Guarantee` or `Alternative Offered` before accepting the order.

## House rules

- Children are allowed only under the relevant room category's occupancy and pricing policy.
- Pets need advance approval and may require an approved pet fee/deposit.
- Smoking is prohibited in rooms; violation creates the published cleaning fee.
- Visitors register with Reception. An overnight visitor requires occupancy amendment and capacity validation.
- Quiet hours are 22:00–08:00.
- Damage is charged at actual repair/replacement cost plus a published cleaning/admin fee where applicable.
- Lost key/access-card and smoking-damage fees use published values.

All fees and restrictions must be published in House Rules and available to the guest before booking confirmation.
