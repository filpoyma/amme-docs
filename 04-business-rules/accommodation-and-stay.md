# Accommodation and Guest Stay

## Check-in and identity

Check-in requires passport or ID, guest name, nationality, date of birth, contact phone, stay details and signed/electronically accepted house rules. Emergency contact is recommended, not universally required. Foreign-guest registration follows applicable local law.

Reception may not check in a guest without ID. A Manager may allow a maximum two-hour temporary exception for a technical/document problem; the exception records reason, deadline and approver.

## Late checkout and extension

| Departure time | Charge |
| --- | ---: |
| By 12:00 | Standard checkout |
| 12:00–14:00 | Free only when no arriving guest needs the room |
| 14:00–18:00 | 50% of the nightly rate |
| After 18:00 | 100% of the nightly rate |

Remaining after 18:00 never creates an automatic new booking. The system creates an extension request and an authorised staff member confirms an extra night only if the room is available.

## Room readiness and inspection

`Room Ready` permits normal check-in when housekeeping has completed cleaning, checked key areas and there is no open maintenance issue. Full inspection is mandatory after maintenance, serious damage, a long vacancy, an incident, an explicit `Needs Inspection` mark, and before VIP or full-villa arrival.

## Minor defects and sellability

A room may be sold with a documented cosmetic defect only when it does not affect safety, hygiene or a key room function and the guest is informed. Examples: minor scratches, wear or decorative defects.

The following are never minor: no hot water, air-conditioning failure, leak, toilet/shower failure, electrical problem, lock failure, mould, insects, water/sewage issue or any safety risk. These require an operational block and cannot be overridden for sale.
