# Booking and Commercial Policy

## Cancellation

| Product | Free cancellation | Partial retention | Full retention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard accommodation | Until 7 days before check-in | 50% of accommodation value inside 7 days | 100% for no-show or cancellation inside 48 hours |
| Group / retreat | Until 30 days before arrival | 50% from 14 to 30 days | 100% inside 14 days |
| Full-villa buyout | Until 60 days before arrival | 50% from 30 to 60 days | 100% inside 30 days |

**Enforcement:** Finance calculates the outcome from the policy snapshot attached to the reservation. The result is a recommendation, not an automatic refund. Any exception requires Manager approval and a reason.

AMME may waive penalties for official force majeure, inability to provide the paid room, or an unresolvable serious room defect. Flight changes and documented illness require Manager review; ordinary personal circumstances do not override the policy.

## Transfer and price protection

- A reservation may be transferred once, to dates within 90 days of the original dates.
- A prepayment transfers in full when the new price is equal to or greater than the original price. The guest pays any increase.
- A lower new price creates no cash refund; it may become service credit if approved.
- The original quoted price applies only to the original dates. New dates use the then-current price.
- A transfer, price, dates, room category, package composition or cancellation-policy change requires recorded guest approval.

## Quote acceptance and booking confirmation

`Quote Accepted` occurs when the guest pays, confirms in writing, signs terms, or the staff records the acceptance evidence. It freezes the version of the commercial offer.

`Booking Confirmed` is a separate manual action by Reception, Manager or Owner. It requires a valid room allocation and the payment condition. No AI action, message, quote acceptance or payment receipt confirms a booking by itself.

## Payment deadline

After a payment deadline, Finance applies a 24-hour grace period. For declared high-demand dates, a Manager may set a 6-hour grace period before the guest accepts the booking terms. At expiry, the obligation becomes `Payment Overdue`; a Manager may then cancel the reservation using reason `payment default`.

## Pricing, discounts and compliments

| Action | Reception | Manager | General Manager / Owner |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Manual price override | Up to 5% | Up to 20% | Above 20% |
| Accommodation discount | Up to 5% | Above 5% | As required by approval limits |
| Complimentary service | Up to 300,000 IDR | Above 300,000 IDR | As required by approval limits |

Every override preserves old and new price, actor, timestamp and reason. A complimentary action requires an approved reason code; it cannot erase the original value.

## Overbooking and downgrade

On an overbooking conflict, the system blocks sale of the affected room and immediately alerts the Manager. Confirmed reservations outrank tentative requests and holds. AMME first finds an internal upgrade; otherwise it provides a comparable-or-better alternative property, pays transfer and price difference, and records guest compensation.

Downgrading a guest requires their consent except in an immediate safety/force-majeure situation. In that exception, the Manager contacts the guest, records an incident, offers an alternative/upgrade and returns the rate difference plus appropriate compensation; serious downgrade guidance is 20–50% of the affected night's price.
